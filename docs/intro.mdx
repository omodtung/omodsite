Welcome to the Blog for building robust, scalable, and high-performance systems as a **Solopreneur**.

This project serves as a blueprint for bridging the gap between rapid development and enterprise-grade reliability, specifically tailored for the **Fintech** landscape.

## Core Philosophy

As a solo developer, you are the entire engineering organization. You need architecture that works for you, not against you.

*   **Scalable Systems First**: Design for growth from day one. Understand distributed systems, asynchronous event-driven patterns, and eventual consistency.
*   **Robust Backend Engineering**: Treat your backend as the backbone of your business. Focus on clean architecture, type safety (TypeScript), and idiomatic implementation.
*   **Fintech Tech Stack**: Master the tools that matter—PostgreSQL for reliable data, Kafka/SQS for event streaming, Kubernetes/EKS for orchestration, and mTLS for uncompromising security.
